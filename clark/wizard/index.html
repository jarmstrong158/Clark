What work happens here?

Only enable tasks your facility actually does every day. Every enabled task consumes worker-hours from your roster — too many enabled tasks on a small roster means the model has to choose between shipping orders and meeting secondary duties, which caps the achievable grade. Pick lean.

Primary tasks (recommended for every facility)

Pick, pack, idle are always on (Clark requires them). Restock + management are strongly recommended unless your facility genuinely doesn't do them.

Optional secondary tasks consumes worker-hours · may demote grades

Only enable these if your facility runs them as a regular daily activity with dedicated worker-hours. Each one you enable subtracts from the hours available for pick/pack/restock/management. On a small roster (≤8 workers), enabling more than 1–2 secondary tasks typically caps you at C-grade because management duty gets starved. Show all 7 optional tasks ↓

Custom tasks (specialized work at your facility)

For work that isn't in the standard list — e.g. "labeling", "kitting", "QC audit". reward_weight scales how much Clark values doing this task (1.0 = same as a unit of shipped orders). Custom tasks defined here will appear as assignable tasks in the workers step.

100 ≈ 7 h — safety margin, modest gains over 50.
200 ≈ 14 h — README's recommended low end, last point with material per-50-eps gains.
